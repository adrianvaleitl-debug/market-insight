Privacy policy, terms & legal information

MarketView is a market research and risk-planning desktop workspace rebuilt with React, TypeScript and Tauri. It does not connect to a brokerage account and does not place trades.

Desktop app data and external connections

The current rebuild stores watchlists, scanner observations, trade plans, price alerts and journal entries in the app's local webview storage. When you refresh market data, the native desktop app sends the ticker symbols you request to Yahoo Finance's chart endpoint and receives public price history and quote information. Yahoo Finance may process the ticker symbols, your IP address, request headers and timestamps under its own terms and privacy practices. No Alpaca credentials or brokerage account connection is included in this rebuild. Price alerts are checked only when you refresh quotes while the app is open. The interface uses system fonts and includes no analytics or advertising SDK. Market data may be delayed or incomplete. The app is a research aid, not financial advice. The project website is hosted through GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary request metadata under GitHub's privacy statement.

Important information about investment-related features

MarketView provides market data displays, technical indicators, scanner results, scores, alerts and planning tools for general research. It does not connect to a brokerage account or execute trades. Outputs are not tailored to your personal circumstances and should not be treated as a personal recommendation to buy, sell or hold an investment. You remain responsible for checking information and making your own decisions.

This wording is not a determination that the software falls outside UK financial-services regulation. Whether any feature, signal, promotion or distribution activity is regulated depends on how it works and is presented in practice. The product's current features should be reviewed by a UK financial-services solicitor or FCA-perimeter specialist before wider promotion or release.
