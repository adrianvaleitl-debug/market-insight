Market Insight is an informational market research and stock-scanning tool. It is not financial advice and does not execute trades.

Desktop app data and external connections

The Windows app stores settings and user-created research information, such as watchlists, scan history, alerts, trade journal entries, plans and notes, in a local settings file under %APPDATA%\\Market Insight. It does not require a hosted account. Market-data requests send the ticker symbols needed for the requested research to Yahoo Finance. If you choose to connect Alpaca, the app sends your API credentials to Alpaca for validation and sends ticker requests for its market-data service; the credentials are stored using Windows Credential Manager, not in the website or a creator-controlled account. Use Settings → Your local data → Delete all Market Insight data to remove local settings and research data; the app also attempts to remove saved Alpaca credentials and warns if Windows cannot confirm removal. Uninstalling the app may leave user data behind, so use the in-app deletion control first. Optional update checks contact GitHub. The reviewed app source contains no analytics or advertising SDK.
